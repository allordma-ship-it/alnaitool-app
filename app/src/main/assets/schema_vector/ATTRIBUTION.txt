Vector schemas (schema_vector/<view>_dark.svg, <view>_light.svg), made by scripts/build_schema_vector.py

The anatomy (myocardium, valves, chambers, sector, probe) is traced as smooth Bézier paths from:
  subcostal_4c, plax_standard, apical_4c, apical_5c, apical_2c, apical_3c, suprasternal_arch:
    view drawings from Echocardiographer.org – Atif Qasim, MD MSCE (free reuse with credit);
  psax_av, psax_papillary:
    "A. Aortic Valve Level" / "C. Mid-Ventricular Level" of the Echocardiographer.org parasternal
    short-axis drawing (tte-psax), set into a sector;
  subcostal_ivc:
    drawn for EchoScope (scripts/reference/subcostal_ivc_drawing.svg).
Colouring (left heart red, right heart blue, dark / light sector) and labels are EchoScope's own.
